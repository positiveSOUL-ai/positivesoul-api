# 🎓 Education & Curriculum — positiveSOUL AI Coach

This folder documents how the **positiveSOUL AI Coach** aligns with Danish *Fælles Mål*, pedagogical principles, and subject-specific teaching support.

Its purpose is to show educators, partners, and auditors *how the AI supports learning safely* without replacing the teacher or producing finished student work.

---

## 📚 Supported School Subjects

The AI Coach currently supports:

### **1. English (Engelsk)**

* Vocabulary development
* Simple sentence formation
* Dialogue practice
* Reading comprehension
* Step-based writing support (no finished texts)
* Classroom tasks with emojis, choices, and guiding questions
* Younger student scaffolding (“stemmesætninger”, simple model sentences)

### **2. Mathematics (Matematik)**

* Step-by-step problem breakdowns
* Concept explanations (fractions, percent, division, multiplication, algebra basics)
* **Never gives final numeric solutions**
* Uses visual metaphors (apples, groups, sharing)
* Encourages the student to produce the final step

### **3. Music (Musik)**

* Rhythm training (body percussion, tempo, ostinato)
* Music theory (notes, scales, intervals, clefs)
* Practical ideas for classroom activities
* Choir & singing warm-ups
* Inspiration for composition and creativity
* Supports *Musik Fælles Mål*: udøvelse, skaben, forståelse

### **4. History (Historie)**

* Time periods, timelines, and event summaries
* Source analysis (kildekritik principles)
* Short factual explanations
* No political opinions — neutral and school-safe
* Supports student reflection instead of providing full essays

## 🎯 Alignment With Fælles Mål

The AI Coach is designed to support—not replace—the teacher's planning and lessons.
All responses follow these core educational principles:

### **1. Guide — do not give**

* No finished assignments
* No essays
* No final math answers
* Always step-based and reflective

### **2. Pedagogical Scaffolding**

* Emojis
* Sentence starters
* Choices (2–3 options)
* “How do you know?” reflection

### **3. Differentiation**

The AI adapts to:

* Age
* Language level
* Teacher or student mode
* Need for structure or flexibility

### **4. Classroom-Safe Guardrails**

* No unsafe topics
* No confidential student advice
* No copyrighted text
* No over-helping (prevents misuse in homework)

## 🧩 Subject Detection & Context Persistence

The backend automatically detects subject based on keywords, emojis, or phrasing.

Examples:

* “fractions”, “÷”, “brøk” → **math**
* “body percussion”, “notes”, “scale” → **music**
* “dialogue”, “vocabulary” → **English**
* “timeline”, “source analysis” → **history**

The subject stays **locked** until the user clearly switches.

---

## 🧪 Teaching Protocol (4 Gears)

1. **Emojis** (supports attention + lowers stress)
2. **Sentence Starters** (“I think…”, “First we…”)
3. **Guiding Questions** (“Which step do you want next?”)
4. **Reflection** (“How do you know?”)

This protocol is embedded in the ruleset and used automatically.
